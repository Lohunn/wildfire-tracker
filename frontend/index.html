How Ember works

01

Geocode

Your saved address is converted to lat/lon via the US Census Geocoder — free, no key required.

02

Detect

NASA FIRMS queries three VIIRS satellites for heat anomalies within your watch radius.

03

Cluster

Nearby simultaneous points are grouped into fire "events" so you don't see duplicate alerts.

04

Enrich

Each cluster is enriched with NWS weather, AirNow AQI, and NIFC official incident data.

05

Score

An explainable 0–100 score is computed with plain‑English reasons — not a black box.

06

Alert

Deduplicated alerts fire on new clusters, satellite confirmations, or score jumps.
